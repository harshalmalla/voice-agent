# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Refunds and Returns
Customers may return unopened items within 30 days of delivery. Refunds are
issued to the original payment method within 5 business days. Items returned
after 30 days are reviewed case by case by the support team.

## Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote
arrangements require director approval and a signed equipment agreement.
Core collaboration hours are 11am to 4pm in your local timezone.

## Expense Reimbursement
Submit expenses within 45 days of purchase through the finance portal.
Meals during travel are reimbursed up to 60 dollars per day. Airfare must be
booked in economy class unless the flight exceeds eight hours.

## Leave
Full time employees accrue 20 days of paid leave per year, plus 10 public
holidays. Unused leave carries over up to a maximum of 5 days into the
next calendar year.
